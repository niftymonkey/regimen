# The judged taxonomy emits an always-on quality signal per category of improvement, an on-shortfall attribution that routes, and reads leverage from deterministic firing plus a targeted audit

> Status: accepted (2026-07-03; proposed 2026-07-01). Supersedes ADR-0008 in part (the single four-value Outcome ordinal) and amends it (the realized signal roster); leaves the rest of ADR-0008 standing. Adopted with all six open questions resolved on the design's recommendations (stamped in `docs/judged-taxonomy-redesign.md` section 10); the build is beginning. Full design in `docs/judged-taxonomy-redesign.md`.

The judged layer must emit the signals that serve the settled categories of improvement (`docs/regimen-categories-of-improvement.md`): Framing, Conducting, Verification, and Leverage, plus the Attribution diagnostic, the real-task gate, and the time and harness/model lenses. ADR-0008 fully specified only two signals (Intent and Outcome) and reserved the rest as a roster whose shapes were TBD. Testing the taxonomy against the driver, and stress-testing a first redo, showed the redo did not survive: it gave Framing and Conducting a home only through an on-shortfall attribution, which masked every quiet case; its attribution was too coarse to represent multi-cause or a kit conflict; and its per-practice adherence signal collided on the `judged_signal` primary key, conflated three actors, and read today's filesystem for every conversation's time. This ADR records the redesign that resolves those, driven top-down from the categories.

## The live-arc categories get always-on quality signals; attribution routes on shortfall

Framing, Conducting, and Verification each get a conversation-scoped, always-on judged signal (`framing`, `conducting`, `verification`) carrying the good, neutral, and poor reads. Always-on is load-bearing: it gives the quiet-but-poor case a home (a session that succeeded yet was poorly conducted drives a real action), it supplies the denominator that makes a trend a rate rather than a count, and it makes the first-class positive "what carried this success" read queryable as a stored positive value rather than an inferred aggregate. Attribution stays what the driver makes it: a conversation-scoped, ON-SHORTFALL categorical diagnostic (`framing / conducting / verification / leverage / ai / environment`) that names the dominant cause, routes to the category that can act, protects from misplaced blame (`ai`, `environment`), and scopes the options. Attribution points; it is never a category's only home. Multi-cause is represented by composing attribution with the always-on category signals that also sit at their poor floor, so no array value and no new value-kind are needed.

## Outcome splits into two axes with a derived spectrum read-key

Outcome ceases to be a single four-value ordinal (this is the part of ADR-0008 superseded). It becomes two orthogonal ordinal axes, `accomplishment` (`not-accomplished < partial < accomplished`, done-ness only) and `correction-cost` (`none < light < heavy`, steering only, emitted when accomplished), whose deterministic correction-rate is its anchor and is never copied into the judged row. The load-bearing worst-to-best spectrum ADR-0008 leaned on survives as a write-time-DERIVED `outcome` value (five ordinal values), stored under the existing `signal_name='outcome'` so the four read sites and the `--outcome` filter that key on it keep returning a scored spectrum with only their value order updated. The derived value is judged-from-judged, not a copied deterministic operand, so ADR-0008's no-copied-operand rule holds. Old verdicts re-project losslessly.

## The gate and the cost read

`engagement` (`engaged / not-engaged`) is the real-task gate and excludes non-tasks from every "fell short" denominator; its `not-engaged` definition drops "a setup blip," which had made a tooling-derailed real task look never-engaged. A minimal `effort` ordinal (`low < moderate < high`, the AI's own grind, deterministically anchorable) is de-parked because the driver names two live cost reads (do-not-delegate-this-class, and retire/loosen a costly lever) that no other signal can feed. The cost reads are consumer compositions of `effort`, `correction-cost`, `accomplishment`, and `intent`; Regimen surfaces the cost and never stores a "worth it" verdict, which is the engineer's call.

## Leverage is a three-part read, not one signal

Adherence is not a per-conversation judged signal. It decomposes by actor and determinism: model-invoked skill FIRING is deterministic (the existing `skill_invocations` table, read by the audit); AI convention-COMPLIANCE is one always-on judged signal (`convention-adherence`: `followed / partially-followed / violated`, abstain when no conventions are in force), which is per-conversation so it has no primary-key collision and gives the consumers a sliceable rate prose cannot; skill APPLICABILITY ("should this non-firing skill have fired here") is an on-demand audit-time judged pass handed the practice's own definition, not an assess signal; and engineer skill USE folds into `conducting` plus firing. Time-scoping is fixed by a new additive `conversation_setup_snapshot` table (roster names and per-convention content hashes captured at the conversation's time), so a bulk re-judge does not fault conversations that predate a lever. This resolves the redo's five adherence failures without a `judged_signal` schema change and with exactly one honest migration.

## Anchors, the generic row, and migration honesty

Every emitted value is anchorable by positive evidence, including positive-evidence-of-skip (`verification=accepted-unverified` cites the change and the accept that bracket the absent check) and the causing event (`attribution=ai` cites the AI's failing output). Every not-applicable state is expressed by ABSTENTION (no row), never by an unanchorable value, so the mandatory-anchor invariant in `buildSignals` stands unchanged and no anchor-optional path is added. All nine emitted signals plus the derived `outcome` fit the existing generic `judged_signal` row: distinct `signal_name`s under `scope='conversation'` (empty-string `assignment_id` sentinel) or `scope='assignment'` (whole-conversation id), so no PKs collide. The only schema change is the additive `conversation_setup_snapshot` migration. This validates ADR-0008's generic row and refutes the redo's false no-migration claim by delivering the shape that actually is near-migration-free.

## Considered options

- **Keep attribution as the sole home for Framing and Conducting (the redo).** Rejected: on-shortfall gating masks the quiet-but-poor case, the trend denominator, and the positive read, re-creating for the live-arc categories the exact masking the redo fixed for Verification.
- **A per-practice always-on adherence signal.** Rejected: per-practice rows collide on the `judged_signal` primary key under insert-or-replace; the only migration-free shape is a structured value needing a new value-kind and hiding counts behind JSON, defeating the SQL-owns-numbers spine; and it conflates firing, applicability, and compliance under one "honored."
- **A keyed adherence table.** Rejected: owns a real migration, re-introduces a practice-id namespace the judged layer never had, and still conflates the three actors.
- **Fold cost/worth into attribution.** Rejected: "well-run but not worth it" is not a shortfall, so it cannot live in an on-shortfall diagnostic; `effort` is always-on and the worth read is a composition, never a stored verdict.
- **Represent multi-cause or the specific recurring pattern as a structured judged value.** Rejected: multi-cause is a composition of existing signals, and a specific pattern or a specific missing lever is open-vocabulary, which would be the escape hatch ADR-0008 forbids; it stays interpretive synthesis in the consumers.
- **Drop the stored `outcome` and derive the spectrum at read time.** Considered and reasonable (no stored redundancy), but the verified read surface is four sites plus a user-facing filter; storing the derived value keeps all of it working with a one-row cost, so read-time derivation is the documented alternative, not the default.

## Consequences

The judge emits `intent`, `accomplishment`, `correction-cost`, a derived `outcome`, `engagement`, `framing`, `conducting`, `verification`, `effort`, `attribution` (on shortfall), and `convention-adherence` (abstain when none in force). The store gains one additive table (`conversation_setup_snapshot`) and no `judged_signal` schema change. The read layer generalizes `rollupHeader` to per-signal distributions so every always-on signal is a trustworthy number, updates the four `outcome`-keyed sites and the `--outcome` filter to the five-value derived spectrum, and reads leverage from deterministic firing plus the snapshot plus `convention-adherence` plus an on-demand applicability pass. The paused branch's setup plumbing and version centralization survive and are reused; `engagement` survives with a wording fix; the four-value Outcome criteria are replaced. The re-sweep (`assess --all --force`) is deferred until this lands, validated on a small sample first, then run once against the redesigned taxonomy. The six open calls recorded in `docs/judged-taxonomy-redesign.md` section 10 (steering-as-axis, effort de-park, convention-adherence as a signal, provenance depth, derived-outcome placement, and elicitation reliability) were resolved by the engineer on 2026-07-03, each on the design's recommendation; the resolutions are stamped in that section.
